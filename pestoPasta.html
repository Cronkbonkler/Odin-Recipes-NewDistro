Pesto Pasta

Violet loves pesto! Her favourite is this green pesto!

Ingredients:

  • 1x Tesco Green Pesto Jar
  • 60g Fusilli Pasta
  • A pinch of salt
  • 1x Hungry Violet!

Method:

  1. Scrub that pan clean so it is not full of filthy vegetables!
  2. Heat 500ml of water in a kettle
  3. Put water in pan and put pan on hob, set to medium heat
  4. Add a small pinch of salt to the water
  5. Once water is boiling add the pasta, make sure to stir it early so it doesn't stick!
  6. Shout "10 minutes Violet!" When you hear the grumbling from the hungry Violet
  7. Cook pasta for 9-12 minutes, stirring occasionally
  8. Drain the water from the pan and open the jar, emptying half of it into the pan
  9. Stir until pasta and pesto are mixed thoroughly
  10. Pour the pasta into a bowl and serve to the hungry, hungry Violet!
