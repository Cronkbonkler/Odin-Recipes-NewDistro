Violet's favourite spaghetti!

A hungry Violet will be thrilled with this yummy spaghetti!

Ingredients:

  • 1x Schwartz Spaghetti Bolognese Mix
  • 350g THIS isn't Mince
  • 1x Olive Oil with FEELING
  • 400g Chopped Tomatoes
  • 2tbps Tomato Puree
  • 1x Brown Onion
  • 60g Tesco Finest Spaghetti
  • 200ml Water
  • 1x Hungry Violet!

Method:

  1. Tell the hungry Violet that she is getting spaghetti bolognese for dinner
  2. Dice that onion!
  3. Cut the mince into chunks
  4. Shove that oil into a (CLEAN) pan and put on a hob. Set to high heat
  5. Cook the mince for 8 minutes and then transfer it to a plate
  6. Cook the onion for 5 minutes or until browned
  7. Add the water, chopped tomatoes, bolognese mix, tomato puree and water to pan
  8. Simmer for 15 minutes
  9. Whilst simmering, tell the hungry Violet that it will be 15 minutes. Endure her complaints.
  10. After 5 minutes, cook the spaghetti according to packet instructions
  11. Drain the spaghetti and add to the pan along with the mince
  12. Stir to coat both whilst shouting "GET READY" to the starving Violet
  13. Transfer dinner to a bowl, put onto a plate and give to the Violet
  14. Watch the hungry Violet finish the bowl in seconds!
