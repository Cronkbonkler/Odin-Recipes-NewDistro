Beans and Sausage!

Violet will demolish this tin in minutes!

Ingredients:

  1. Make sure your pan is squeaky clean! Violet will not like it if stinky spices or carrots are stuck in there!
  2. Open the tin of Beans and Sausage (Do not let Violet smell the tin or she will NOT leave you alone!)
  3. Pour the contents of the tin into the pan.
  4. Turn on the hob and set to a low/medium heat. It will cook quickly, so tell that Violet that is probably creeping through the door that she won't have to wait long!
  5. Cook for 3 to 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, breaking the sausages in half as you go.
  6. Tell Violet to get ready!
  7. Pour the Beans and Sausage into a (warmed) bowl and put the bowl on a small plate.
  8. Give that starving Violet her food!!!
